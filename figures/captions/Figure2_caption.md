**Figure 2. Dataset composition and representation of the operational reef-fish ontology.** (A) Pre-augmentation partitions comprised 8,517 training source images, 698 validation images, and 520 held-out test images; bar lengths use a square-root display. Training augmentation produced four outputs per source image (34,068 exported training files), not additional independent source photographs. (B) Pre-augmentation training support for 51 fish taxon/morphotype categories and the auxiliary `unidentifiable` category, ordered by total support and separated by SAMP and iNaturalist provenance. Image source was assigned from filenames: names beginning with `2024` or `2025` were classified as SAMP, and all remaining names as iNaturalist. The horizontal axis uses a square-root display. Support counts are numbers of source images containing each class and need not sum to the number of distinct training images because an image may contain multiple classes. (C) Representative original SAMP photographs illustrating species-level, pooled-taxon, initial-phase, and `unidentifiable` levels of the operational image-based ontology. A thin neutral ellipse in the final tile indicates the intended illustrative individual; it is not a model output. Panel C illustrates label granularity and does not evaluate model performance. The iNaturalist contribution in panel B describes dataset composition; it does not establish a causal effect on detector performance.
