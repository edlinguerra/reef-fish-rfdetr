**Figure 4. Class-level error structure of the selected detector.** (A) Corrected row-normalized assignments for 52 operational taxon/morphotype classes; the final “No match” column represents unmatched ground-truth objects. (B) Partition of 1,846 annotated ground-truth objects into correct-class matches, wrong-class matches, and unmatched objects. Wrong-class matches are localized detections assigned to another operational class, whereas unmatched objects have no matched prediction. These diagnostics use saved confidence-filtered predictions and the specified one-to-one, best-unused matching procedure at IoU ≥ 0.50; their counts are distinct from the numerators of the primary RF-DETR precision and recall metrics.
