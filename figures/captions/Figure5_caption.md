**Figure 5. Distribution of image-level detection performance in visual feature space.** (A, B) The same UMAP coordinates for all 520 test images, colored by per-image F1 and false-negative (FN) rate, respectively. Hollow gray symbols denote images without ground-truth objects, for which these metrics are undefined rather than zero. Among 490 ground-truth-containing images, 83 had F1 = 0 and 95 had FN rate ≥ 0.75. (C) Observed-to-expected same-group-neighbor ratios and permutation p-values for low-F1 and high-FN images in the 512-dimensional CLIP and projected UMAP spaces (15 nearest neighbors). Horizontal segments connect each observed ratio to the null reference of 1.0; they are not uncertainty intervals. No significant local enrichment was detected under these specified tests; this does not establish the causes of individual detection failures.
